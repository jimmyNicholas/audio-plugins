# Business plan

How the plugins and devices in this repo could make money, at hobby scale. Last updated 2026-11-09.

## Summary

Release work in **albums**: themed sets of small, cheap, unique plugins, each with a free browser version, released alongside music made with them and a sample pack made from them. Everything lives on **my own website**, where the story and design philosophy behind each tool are told; that story is the marketing and discovery, and it bridges the music and software audiences. People buy **singles, albums, or the whole discography**, all cheaply, through a **rented checkout** (itch.io behind the site to start) so tax and delivery are handled elsewhere. Music and sample packs also go on **Bandcamp**. Windows first, Mac when there's demand. Light copy protection: one signed licence file per buyer. Releases are regular but not scheduled: an album goes out when its checklist passes. Fixed costs are about A$0 a year beyond the domain until Mac is added (about A$150 a year).
