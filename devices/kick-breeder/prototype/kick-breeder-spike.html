Kick Breeder spike

The question: if you choose between kicks round after round, with new ones bred from each choice, do you land on a kick you like within about ten rounds? Every sound here is synthesised; there are no samples.

Press 1 2 3 to hear a slot, Enter to choose the slot you are listening to, Space to start or stop the drum loop.

The loop plays hats and claps with the kick from the slot you are listening to, so you can judge it in a beat.
